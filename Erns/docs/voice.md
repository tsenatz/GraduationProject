# Voice

Tone: measured, direct, calm, formal.

- Keep sentences around 16 words; that is the measured average across the site's copy.
- Address the reader as "you" (about 6.9 times per 100 words on the site).
- Avoid exclamation marks; the measured copy almost never uses them.
- Label buttons with short nouns or phrases, about 2 words long.

## Examples (new sentences in this voice)

- **headline**: Everything your team needs to plan, build and ship in one place.
- **button**: Create your first project
- **empty state**: You haven't added anything yet. Your first item will appear here.
- **error**: We couldn't save your changes. Check your connection and try again.
- **subheading**: Built For Teams That Move Fast
